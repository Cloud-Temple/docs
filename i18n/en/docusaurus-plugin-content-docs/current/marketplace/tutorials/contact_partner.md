This tutorial guides you through contacting a publisher partner via the Cloud Temple Marketplace.

## Usage Context

Use this feature when:

- The solution requires connecting with the partner
- You need additional information
- The solution is not directly deployable (no VM Image)

## Contact Steps

### 1. Form Access

From the partner solution details page, click **Contact the partner** :
<img src={marketplaceContactAccess} />

### 2. Sending the request

Validate and send your contact request:
<img src={marketplaceContactConfirm} />

You will receive an email confirmation upon submission of your request.

The partner will automatically receive an email with your information:

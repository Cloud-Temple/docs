# AIOps overview

Cloud Temple's **AIOps** service is an operational intelligence platform: it applies artificial intelligence to the monitoring, incident analysis and automation of the operations of your information system. It assists your teams like a copilot, without replacing them.

AIOps is a managed service. It is subscribed through Cloud Temple experts, who study with you its integration into your environment: it is not ordered from the Cloud Temple Console and has neither a view nor an API there.

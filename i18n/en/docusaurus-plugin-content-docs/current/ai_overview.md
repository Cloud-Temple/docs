# AI

Cloud Temple's artificial intelligence services let you use AI models on a sovereign infrastructure hosted in France. To run your own workloads on NVIDIA GPUs, see the GPU blades of [Bare Metal](./iaas_bare-metal/concepts.md#gpu), [IaaS VMware](./iaas_vmware/concepts.md#gpu) and [IaaS OpenSource](./iaas_opensource/concepts.md#gpu), and the GPU worker nodes of [PaaS OpenShift](./paas_openshift/concepts.md#gpu).

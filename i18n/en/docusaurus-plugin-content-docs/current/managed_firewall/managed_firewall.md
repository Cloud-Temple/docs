# Managed Firewall

**Managed Firewall** is a fully managed next-generation firewall (NGFW), deployed as a high-availability cluster. It filters traffic between the Internet and your cloud resources as well as between your internal network segments, protects against intrusions and provides VPN connectivity to your remote sites and mobile users.

The service is offered in two editions, Stormshield and Fortinet. Deployment, high availability, updates and monitoring are handled for you: your teams focus on the security policy.
